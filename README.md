## 🎯 Business Problem & Purpose

Procurement teams and financial managers often struggle to reconcile supply volumes with active spending and supplier reliability. This solution transforms raw purchasing logs into dynamic visual analytics to help stakeholders:
- Track vendor fulfillment and cost distribution.
- Identify cash flow commitments and outstanding vendor liabilities.
- Optimize procurement budget allocation across core purchasing categories.

---

## 📈 Key Metrics & Features

- **Executive KPI Cards:** 
  - **Total Orders:** Tracks 500 completed purchase orders.
  - **Total Volume:** Monitors 26K units supplied.
  - **Total Spend:** $26M overall procurement cost.
  - **Pending Liabilities:** Identifies $10.78M in unpaid balances.
  - **Payment Fulfillment Rate:** Tracks % Paid status (23.41%).
- **Monthly Spend Trend (Line Chart):** Analyzes cost fluctuations over time to spot seasonal purchasing spikes (e.g., Q1 vs. Q4).
- **Category Cost Breakdown (Donut Chart):** Evaluates spend distribution by product types (Electronics at 61.31%, Furniture, Consumables).
- **Supplier Volume Ranking (Horizontal Bar Chart):** Ranks vendors by total billing (Supplier D, A, B leading total cost share).
- **Geographical Distribution (Interactive Map Visual):** Displays procurement volume across North American vendor locations.
- **Dynamic Slicer Controls:** Top filter bar allowing interactive slicing by Month, Supplier, Location, Category, and Payment Status.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Stack & Implementation

- **Data Visualization Tool:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- **Data Modeling & Calculations:** DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) for dynamic aggregations, unpaid balances, and % fulfillment metrics.
- **UI/UX Design Concept:** Modern Container-style layout using a custom purple-accent color scheme designed for executive readability.
